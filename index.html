How do I book a Pandit ji for my ritual?

Booking is very simple. Choose your desired service from our "Core Rituals" section, click "Book Now", and fill in your basic details. The request will be sent to our WhatsApp, and our team will instantly confirm your booking and Pandit ji's availability.

Is the Puja Samagri included in the booking?

Yes! We offer fully inclusive packages. Our experienced Brahmins will bring all the authentic and pure Puja Samagri required for your specific ritual, so you can focus entirely on your prayers without any hassle.

Do you provide Brahmins based on specific regional traditions?

Absolutely. We understand that every region has its unique Vedic traditions. We provide specialized Pandits including Marwadi, Telugu, Bengali, and North Indian Brahmins to ensure your ritual is conducted exactly as per your family's customs.

How are the payments handled?

Once your booking is confirmed over WhatsApp, we will share the payment details. You can pay securely via UPI, Bank Transfer, or Cash upon completion of the ritual, depending on the service package you choose.
